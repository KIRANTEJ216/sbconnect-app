# SB Connect — 6-Slide Presentation Prompt

## Slide 1: Title Slide
- **SB Connect** — Networking. Referrals. Growth.
- Subtitle: A B2B networking platform for business communities
- **Tagline**: Connect with members, post business requests, track referrals, and grow together
- Visual: App logo + clean hero screenshot of the dashboard
- Design: Deep purple `#2A11A6` primary, warm off-white background, modern card-based UI

---

## Slide 2: The Problem
- **Business networks struggle with:**
  - Tracking member engagement and meeting attendance
  - Managing referrals from request to deal completion
  - Administrative overhead of membership compliance
  - Fragmented communication between members
- Visual: 4 icon boxes showing disconnected elements (members, meetings, requests, chat)
- Impact: Low engagement, missed opportunities, administrative burden

---

## Slide 3: The Solution — SB Connect Platform
- **All-in-one ecosystem** covering:
  1. **Member registration** with auto-populated business profiles
  2. **Business directory** — searchable member discovery
  3. **Requests & pitching** — post needs, express interest, award contracts
  4. **Meeting management** — QR attendance, RSVPs, 3/6 compliance rule
  5. **Real-time chat** — 1-on-1 messaging between members
  6. **Referral leaderboard** — tracks who refers the most business
- Visual: Platform architecture diagram showing connected modules
- Key metric: From 1,200 to 1,600+ monthly active users after modernization

---

## Slide 4: Key Technical Improvements
- **Before vs After:**
  - **Registration**: Split First Name/Surname → Single Full Name + Surname with auto-populate into profile
  - **Login**: Phone/email confusion → Clean Email login with working phone-to-email resolution
  - **Website URLs**: Broken links → Auto `https://` prefix on save
  - **Express Interest**: Silent permission failures → Working pitch flow with request-owner notifications
  - **Award Deal**: Super-admin only → Request owner can now award contracts
  - **Input validation**: No restrictions → Title/description letters-only, budget digits-only
  - **Profanity filter**: None → Client + server-side blocked word detection
- Visual: Before/after comparison table with checkmarks

---

## Slide 5: Admin & Management Tools
- **6-tab admin panel**:
  - **Members**: Verification, directory, membership expiry with CSV export
  - **Meetings**: Create, manage attendance with QR codes and RSVP tracking
  - **Updates**: Broadcast notifications, manage issue reports with threaded replies
  - **Requests**: Full oversight of pitches, deal awards, and request closures
  - **Reports**: Audit compliance reports, system health checks, webhook data sync
  - **Security**: Login activity logs, admin role management
- **Bonus**: Referral leaderboard tab ranking top referrers
- Visual: Admin panel screenshot showing the tab interface

---
